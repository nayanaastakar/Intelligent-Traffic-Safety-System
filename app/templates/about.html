Intelligent Traffic Safety System (ITSS)

Comprehensive documentation, architectural workflows, neural pipelines, and technical specifications.

📋 Project Overview & Problem Statement

Modern roadway networks face escalating challenges from exponential vehicle density, human driver fatigue, delayed emergency accident responses, and speeding infractions. Traditional manual traffic monitoring is labor-intensive, error-prone, and incapable of continuous real-time incident detection.

The Intelligent Traffic Safety System transforms existing roadway surveillance cameras into autonomous safety sentinels. By leveraging state-of-the-art computer vision models (Darknet YOLOv3 and Google MediaPipe), the platform delivers automated vehicle counting, congestion classification, collision boundary detection, speed tracking overlays, and operator fatigue monitoring in a unified command center.

🏗️ System Architecture & Data Flow

STEP 1 Media Intake Layer: Video streams, uploaded MP4/AVI surveillance feeds, high-res roadway imagery, or live webcams.
STEP 2 Neural Inference Layer: OpenCV DNN forward-pass through YOLOv3-Tiny (416x416 blob) + MediaPipe 468-point 3D FaceMesh.
STEP 3 Spatial & Temporal Evaluation: Centroid tracking, rolling density coefficients, bounding box overlap checks with delayed confirmation.
STEP 4 Telemetry & Alert Generation: Persistent SQLite session logs, critical acoustic alarms, annotated video compilation, and evidence frames.

🚀 Phase 14: Future Computer Vision Enhancements

1. Automated Number Plate Recognition (ANPR)
High-accuracy OCR for license plates with data privacy anonymization.
2. Lane-wise Density & Heatmaps
Delineated corridor traffic occupancy and spatial density heatmaps.
3. Two-Wheeler Helmet Detection
Motorcyclist safety compliance detection for helmet and safety gear.
4. Emergency Vehicle Priority Classification
Ambulance and fire engine detection for automated green-wave traffic signaling.

💻 Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python 3.11, Flask 3.0, Werkzeug
  • Computer Vision: OpenCV 4.13, MediaPipe 1.2, NumPy
  • Deep Learning: Darknet YOLOv3-Tiny (48 layers)
  • Database: SQLite with SQLAlchemy ORM
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js
  • Video Processing: H.264 / MP4V codecs, MJPEG streaming

🧩 Core CV Modules

1. Vehicle Classifier: Classifies cars, trucks, buses, motorcycles, bicycles.
2. Traffic Density: Rolling frame vehicle count classified into Low, Medium, High.
3. Accident Proximity: Spatial bounding box intersection with frame persistence.
4. Overspeed Radar: Optical centroid displacement & limit comparison HUD.
5. Driver Drowsiness: Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) using 6 landmark coordinates per eye.

⚠️ System Limitations

• Speed estimation uses 2D pixel motion; accurate km/h requires surveyed roadway geometry and camera calibration.
• Optical accident flags are based on proximity overlap and require human operator confirmation before dispatch.
• Drowsiness detection requires direct facial line-of-sight and adequate in-cabin lighting.
